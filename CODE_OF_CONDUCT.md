# Code of Conduct

Capital Ark welcomes contributors across political viewpoints, identities, and
levels of experience. The project is non-partisan; participation does not imply
agreement about a candidate, party, industry, or policy.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people or presumed motives.
- Make space for questions and different levels of technical experience.
- Protect contributor and user privacy, especially around political activity.
- Disclose conflicts of interest relevant to a proposed candidate, committee,
  processor, vendor, or policy change.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on project scope without harassment, even when
  you disagree.

## Unacceptable behavior

- harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, discrimination, or unwanted
  sexual attention;
- personal attacks, deliberate misgendering, slurs, or degrading imagery;
- publishing or threatening to publish private information;
- coordinated political pressure, spam, or campaign messaging unrelated to the
  technical work;
- manipulating activity, candidate data, or contribution totals; and
- retaliation against someone who raises a good-faith concern.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions,
project events, and private communications made in an official project role.
It also applies when someone publicly represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through a contact method on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/PatrickSean). If no private
method is available, open an issue containing only "Private conduct contact
requested" and no personal or incident details. A maintainer will arrange a
private channel.

Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as reasonably possible. Maintainers
may remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or
permanently ban a participant based on impact and pattern. A maintainer who is
the subject of a report must not decide its outcome; another trusted reviewer
should handle it whenever possible.

No policy can promise perfect confidentiality or a fixed response time. Do not
include contribution receipts, donor data, credentials, or unrelated private
information in a report.
